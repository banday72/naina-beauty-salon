Good to Know

Frequently asked
questions

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking at least a day in advance — especially for bridal and party makeup, which need extra time and preparation.

Is the salon ladies-only?

Yes. NAINA Beauty Salon is a fully ladies-only salon. The entire space is staffed by and reserved for female clients, so you can relax in complete comfort.

Do you offer services at home?

Yes! We offer bridal, facial, mehndi and mani pedi services at your home. Availability depends on your location — mention "Home Service" when booking or message us on WhatsApp to confirm.

How does the wedding package work?

Our 3-day package covers your Mehndi, Baraat and Walima looks. Pick your Mehndi (start) date and Walima (end) date in the booking form and we'll reserve all your wedding days exclusively for you.

What are your timings?

We're open every day from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. For very early bridal appointments, please call us and we'll arrange a special time.
